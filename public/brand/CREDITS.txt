HOW BACKGROUNDS WORK  (src/components/PhotoFrame.astro)
  Hero bands are ~3.6:1 on desktop and the job photos are 3:4 portraits, so
  cropping them to fill the band always cut off the key, fob or badge. Each
  frame is instead two layers:

    brand/fill/<slug>.webp   the same shot at 120px, blurred and darkened,
                             stretched across the band (~500 bytes each)
    /work/<slug>.webp        the real photograph, WHOLE and uncropped, in
                             colour, with its descriptive alt text — the same
                             URL listed in /image-sitemap.xml

  PhotoFrame throws at build time if handed a stock or AI image.
