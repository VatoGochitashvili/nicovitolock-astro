ALL BACKGROUND PHOTOGRAPHY IS PROCESSED INTO THE BRAND DUOTONE
Navy shadows (#0a1822) to warm highlight (#e9e2d4), baked into the file rather
than applied with CSS filters. Zero runtime cost, identical in every browser,
and it is what stops a stock photograph from looking like a stock photograph —
it arrives already in the palette. A light Gaussian blur is applied because
these sit behind a ~77% scrim where fine detail is invisible but expensive:
it roughly halved the file sizes and improved text legibility.

Scrim levels are measured, not guessed. Against the brightest 95th-percentile
patch in each image's text column, body copy clears WCAG AA at 8.7:1 or better
and the brass accent at 5.5:1 or better. Re-measure before lowering them.
