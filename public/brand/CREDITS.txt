NO STOCK PHOTOGRAPHY
  This site deliberately uses none. The earlier build borrowed Brooklyn stock
  shots from a sibling project (locksandcameras.com); they were removed because
  shared imagery made the two sites look like the same template. Everything
  visual here is Nico & Vito's own.

TO ADD YOUR OWN
  Drop photos into public/brand/ and reference them from the components.
  Worth shooting: the shop/van on 3rd Avenue, a tech at a door, key cutting in
  the van, the Verrazzano from Shore Road. Real local photos beat stock for
  trust and for local SEO.
